    Our advice

    Which one fits Noor te Nakhra?

    Choose Shopify if

    • You want to start selling before the wedding and festive season
    • Monthly online sales are still small or uncertain
    • Your team wants to add products and run sales without help

    Choose Custom if

    • You want the lowest total cost, with no monthly platform or app fees
    • You want features no theme offers, like custom stitching orders
    • You want to own the code and data with no platform commission
    Payment & terms

    How payments work

    • All prices are in Indian Rupees.
    • Third-party costs (Shopify membership, apps, domain, hosting) are paid directly by the client. Shopify adds GST to its membership fee.
    • Product photos, descriptions and prices are provided by the client. We upload up to 100 products.
    • Includes 3 rounds of design changes and 3 months of free support after launch.
