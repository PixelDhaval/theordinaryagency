# How we work

The same four-step process runs under every project — branding, packaging, pitch decks, social, brochures, UI/UX. What changes is what happens inside each step.

---

## 1. Discovery

We start by understanding your goals, audience, and the scope of what you need. For branding, that means your positioning and story. For UI/UX, it means your users and their flows. For packaging, it means your product and where it'll be seen.

## 2. Concept

We explore a small number of strong directions rather than dozens of weak ones — so your feedback at this stage is focused and useful, not overwhelming.

## 3. Design & Refinement

We build out the chosen direction in full and refine it based on your feedback. This is where most of the back-and-forth happens, and where the work gets sharp.

## 4. Delivery & Handoff

You receive final files, guidelines, or documentation — whatever's needed to put the work to use. Packaging projects get production-ready files; UI/UX projects get developer handoff; branding projects get a guidelines document.

---

## What we need from you

- A clear sense of your goals and audience
- Any existing brand assets, if you have them
- Timely feedback during review rounds — this is the biggest factor in keeping a project on schedule
