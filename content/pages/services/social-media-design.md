## Our process

### 1. Discovery & Brand Review
We look at your existing brand (or build from your branding project) and your current social presence.

### 2. Template & Style Direction
We design the visual direction and a small set of flexible templates.

### 3. Design Production
We build out the full set of templates and/or campaign graphics.

### 4. Handoff
You receive editable files or a ready-to-use template system.
