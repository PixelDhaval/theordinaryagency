# Packaging design services

Packaging is the first physical moment someone has with your brand — on a shelf or on their doorstep. We design packaging that holds its own there, then hand off everything your printer needs.

---

## What's included

- Concept directions and structural input
- Graphic design across all panels
- Print-ready files with correct bleed and color specs
- Dieline coordination with your printer

---

## Who this is for

- **A new product launching** and needing packaging from scratch.
- **An existing line refreshing** its look without losing what customers already recognize.
- **A multi-SKU product family** that needs a consistent system across variants.

---

## Our process

### 1. Discovery & Research
We learn the product, the shelf or unboxing context, and what competitors are doing.

### 2. Concept Directions
We explore a small number of strong directions, grounded in how the packaging will actually be seen and used.

### 3. Design Development
We build out the chosen direction across every panel and variant.

### 4. Production-Ready Handoff
You receive print-ready files, correctly set up for your printer, plus a digital version.
