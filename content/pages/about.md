## How we think about design

Minimal doesn't mean empty — it means nothing is on the page (or the package, the deck, the screen) that isn't earning its place. We'd rather spend time removing what doesn't matter than adding what might. That's the standard we hold every branding, packaging, pitch deck, social, brochure, and UI/UX project to.
