## Content Brief: design agency portfolio

### Search Intent
Mostly navigational/branded (visitors coming from the homepage or service pages to see proof), with a smaller commercial-research segment specifically searching for portfolio examples before hiring. Google format reward: visual grid/index page linking to deep case studies, not long-form text. Competitive SERP relevance for the bare term is low (dominated by inspiration-roundup sites like Pafolios, Awwwards); this brief prioritizes on-site conversion structure over ranking for the generic term.

### Competitor Analysis
| # | URL | Key H2 Sections | Est. Words | Score | Main Gap |
|---|-----|-----------------|------------|-------|----------|
| 1 | ramotion.com/work/ | Filterable project grid by discipline | low (visual-led) | 27/40 | Strong visual grid but minimal framing copy — works for an established brand, less so for a new studio needing more trust-building context |
| 2 | fiercecreative.agency/portfolio | Case studies in branding & design | low | 22/40 | Project cards lack clear outcome/result framing per case |

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** Best-practice guidance (confirmed across multiple sources) is that strong case studies state the client's goal, the challenge, the studio's role, the approach, and the result — many competitor grids skip straight to images with no framing. Adding even a one-line result/outcome per project card is a gap most portfolios miss.
- **Depth gap:** Filtering by discipline (branding, packaging, pitch deck, social, brochure, UI/UX) matches how this specific studio is organized — most competitor portfolios filter generically ("branding", "web", "mobile") without matching a studio's exact service menu.

### Site Structure Coverage Rule
As an index/hub page, filtering/categorization should map to all six live service categories so each has a path into relevant case studies: Branding, Packaging Design, Pitch Deck Design, Social Media Design, Brochure Design, UI/UX Design.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Our Work
**URL Slug:** /work
**Target Word Count:** ~400-600 words (intentionally visual-led; case studies carry the depth, not this index)

- **Intro (40-60 words):** One short line framing the body of work — primary keyword "portfolio" used naturally once.
- **Filter/category bar (no copy, functional):** Filter by the six service categories.
- **Project grid (short captions only, ~15-25 words per project):** Each card: project name, client/industry, discipline tag, one-line outcome. Links to /work/{slug}.
- **Closing CTA (40-60 words):** Link to /contact, framed as "start your project."

### Recommended Meta Tags

**Title**
Our Work | Branding, Packaging & UI/UX Case Studies

**Meta Description**
Branding, packaging, pitch deck, social, brochure, and UI/UX projects — see the work and the thinking behind it, then start your own.

### Unique Angle and Information Gain
A one-line, specific outcome per project card (not just an image) — a small but real gap versus competitor grids that show visuals with zero context, making it hard for a visitor to judge relevance to their own situation at a glance.

### E-E-A-T Requirements
- Only real, completed client projects shown — no concept/speculative work presented as client work without being labeled as such
- Outcome lines must be accurate, not inflated (e.g. don't claim "300% growth" without verified data)

### Internal Linking Opportunities
- Each project card → its own case study page /work/{slug} (anchor: project/client name)
- "Explore our services" → /services (anchor: "see our services")
- CTA → /contact (anchor: "start your project")
