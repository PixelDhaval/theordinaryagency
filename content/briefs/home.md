## Content Brief: branding and design agency

### Search Intent
Mixed commercial/navigational. Searchers are either evaluating agencies to hire ("branding and design agency") or already know the brand and are confirming services/credibility before enquiring. Audience: founders, marketing leads, and brand managers at small-to-mid-size businesses who need multiple design disciplines (branding, packaging, pitch decks, social, brochures, UI/UX) from one partner instead of juggling freelancers. Google rewards a clean landing-page format here: short value proposition, service overview, proof (case studies/clients), and a direct CTA - not a long-form article.

Note on realism: the bare term "design agency" is dominated by global players (Pentagram, IDEO) and directories (Clutch, DesignRush, Dribbble) that a new site cannot outrank quickly. This brief targets the more winnable, intent-matched variant "branding and design agency" and treats broader reach as a long-term goal driven by the service pages, not the homepage alone.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** None of the reviewed homepages tie together *all six* of this agency's specific disciplines (branding, packaging, pitch decks, social, brochures, UI/UX) under one clear narrative - most either go broad-digital (web/dev-heavy) or narrow (branding-only, packaging-only). A homepage that explicitly frames "one studio, every brand touchpoint" is a real gap.
- **Depth gap:** Competitors front-load stats and awards but give shallow, generic value propositions ("end-to-end", "all-in-one"). There's room to lead with a sharper, more specific promise tied to minimal/elegant craft rather than scale.
- **Quality gap:** Several competitor homepages are visually dense with FAQs and long case-study grids, working against a minimal aesthetic. A homepage that stays short, visual, and confident (letting work speak) is a differentiator for a design-forward brand.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Branding and Design Agency for Brands That Want to Stand Out
**URL Slug:** /
**Target Word Count:** ~650-750 words (competitor avg ~3,500; intentionally shorter - minimal design relies on visuals and whitespace, not copy volume)

- **Hero (40-60 words):** One clear value statement naming the agency and core promise. Primary keyword "branding and design agency" in the first sentence. Single CTA button to /contact.
- **Services strip (100-120 words, FS target: service snippet):** Six service categories as visual cards (Branding, Packaging, Pitch Decks, Social Media Design, Brochures, UI/UX), each linking to its service page. Secondary keywords: "brand identity design", "packaging design", "pitch deck design" used once each as card labels.
- **Featured work (60-80 words + visuals):** 3-6 curated case study thumbnails linking to /work/{slug}. Caption each with client + discipline, no long copy.
- **Why this studio / philosophy (120-150 words):** Short paragraph on design philosophy (minimal, elegant, outcome-driven). This is the section to carry brand voice and differentiate from the generic "end-to-end" competitor language.
- **Process teaser (60-80 words):** 3-4 step micro-summary linking to /process.
- **Social proof (80-100 words):** Client logos and/or 1-2 short testimonials. If none exist yet, launch without this section rather than inventing claims.
- **Closing CTA (40-60 words):** Direct line + button to /contact ("Book a call").

### Unique Angle and Information Gain
Lead with the specific combination of disciplines this studio actually offers (not a generic "full-service" claim) and prove it with real, specific case studies rather than stats pulled from nowhere. The gap in the market is a homepage that reads as curated and minimal rather than a dense agency directory listing - fewer words, stronger visual proof, one clear next step.

### E-E-A-T Requirements
- Real client names/logos only where permission exists (omit the section if none are available yet rather than using placeholders)
- Case study thumbnails must link to real, detailed case studies (not mockups)
- Clear, verifiable contact path (real booking link, real email)
- If testimonials are used, they must be attributable to a real client, not written in-house

### Internal Linking Opportunities
- "See our services" → /services (anchor: "explore our design services")
- Each service card → its own service page (anchor: exact service name, e.g. "Packaging design")
- "View our work" → /work (anchor: "see recent projects")
- "How we work" → /process (anchor: "our process")
- CTA → /contact (anchor: "book a call")
