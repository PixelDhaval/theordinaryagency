## Content Brief: pitch deck design services

### Search Intent
Commercial/transactional with urgency - searchers often have a fundraising deadline or investor meeting approaching. Audience: startup founders and business leaders needing an investor- or sales-ready deck. Google rewards a service/landing-page format emphasizing speed, outcomes (funding raised), and visual sample work.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** Few competitors clearly separate "content/story help" from "design-only" engagements. Stating plainly whether this studio helps with narrative/content or purely executes design on a client's existing content is a clarity gap worth closing.
- **Depth gap:** Halo Lab's FAQ covers static vs. animated decks and NDA/confidentiality - both are real buyer concerns (investors often require confidentiality) that smaller competitors skip entirely.
- **Quality gap:** Competitors lean heavily on funding-raised figures as the core proof point. A studio without verifiable large-scale figures should lead with deck quality/visual proof and process clarity instead of borrowing unverifiable stats.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Pitch Deck Design Services
**URL Slug:** /services/pitch-deck-design
**Target Word Count:** ~1,200-1,400 words (competitor avg ~6,500; far shorter, visual sample-led)

- **Intro (60-80 words):** What's offered - design-only vs. design+narrative support should be stated clearly here. Primary keyword in first sentence.
- **What's included (150-180 words, bullet list):** Slide design and layout, data visualization/charts, brand-aligned visual system, editable source files (e.g. Figma/PowerPoint/Keynote).
- **Who this is for (80-100 words):** Fundraising founders, sales teams needing a pitch refresh, teams presenting at a board/investor meeting.
- **Process (180-220 words, 4 steps):** Discovery & content review → Structure & storyboard → Design & visualization → Delivery & presenter support.
- **Case studies (100-150 words + visuals):** Deck sample thumbnails/slides from /work.
- **FAQ (150-200 words, 4-5 Qs, FS target):** Turnaround time, confidentiality/NDA handling, whether content/copywriting is included, file formats delivered, revision rounds.
- **CTA (40-50 words):** Link to /contact.
