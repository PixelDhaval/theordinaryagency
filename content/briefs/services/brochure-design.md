## Content Brief: brochure design services

### Search Intent
Commercial. Searchers typically need printed (or print-style digital) collateral for sales, events, or informational handouts. Audience: small business owners, sales teams, and marketers needing physical or PDF collateral. Google rewards a service/landing-page format with clear deliverables and format options (tri-fold, bi-fold, sales sheet).

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** SmartSites covers copywriting as part of the offering - many competitors assume the client supplies all copy. Stating clearly whether copywriting support is included (or copy must be client-provided) is a gap most pages leave ambiguous.
- **Depth gap:** Format options (tri-fold, bi-fold, multi-page, single-sheet) are mentioned by name on SmartSites but rarely explained - a short explanation of which format suits which use case (trade show vs. mailer vs. leave-behind) adds real decision value.
- **Quality gap:** Competitor proof points are often agency-wide (total revenue generated, review counts) rather than brochure-specific - a page with brochure-specific case studies is more credible for this exact service.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Brochure Design Services
**URL Slug:** /services/brochure-design
**Target Word Count:** ~1,200-1,400 words (competitor avg ~3,500; tighter)

- **Intro (60-80 words):** What's offered, and whether copywriting is included. Primary keyword in first sentence.
- **Format options (120-150 words, FS target: short definition list):** Tri-fold, bi-fold, multi-page booklet, single-sheet - one line each on best use case.
- **What's included (120-150 words, bullet list):** Layout and design, brand-consistent typography/imagery, print-ready files (bleed, color profile) plus a digital/PDF version.
- **Who this is for (80-100 words):** Trade show handouts, sales leave-behinds, mailers, informational one-pagers.
- **Process (150-200 words, 4 steps):** Discovery (goal, audience, format) → Copy & content (client-provided or supported) → Design execution → Print-ready + digital delivery.
- **Case studies (100-150 words + visuals):** Brochure samples from /work.
- **FAQ (120-150 words, 3-4 Qs):** Turnaround time, printing handled or handoff-only, revision rounds, whether digital-only versions are available.
- **CTA (40-50 words):** Link to /contact.
