## Content Brief: UI/UX design services

### Search Intent
Commercial. Searchers are evaluating design partners for a digital product (website, app, SaaS interface). Audience: founders/product leads who need interface design, ranging from early-stage (need an MVP designed) to established (need a redesign or design system). Google rewards a service/landing-page format emphasizing process, platform coverage, and visual/product proof.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** Few competitors clearly distinguish UX (research, flows, information architecture) from UI (visual interface design) for a buyer who doesn't know the difference - a short plain-language explanation is a real gap.
- **Depth gap:** Swovo lists many engagement models (staff augmentation, embedded teams) that suit enterprise buyers; a project-based studio should state its actual engagement model plainly instead of copying enterprise flexibility claims it can't support.
- **Quality gap:** Most competitor UI/UX pages assume a SaaS/enterprise product. Stating clearly which product types are supported (web app, mobile app, marketing site, SaaS dashboard) avoids mismatched enquiries.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** UI/UX Design Services
**URL Slug:** /services/ui-ux-design
**Target Word Count:** ~1,400-1,600 words (competitor avg ~4,500; tighter, more direct)

- **Intro (60-80 words):** What's offered and the actual engagement model (project-based vs. retainer). Primary keyword in first sentence.
- **UX vs. UI, plainly explained (100-130 words):** Short explainer distinguishing research/flows (UX) from visual interface design (UI) - builds buyer literacy and trust.
- **What's included (150-200 words, bullet list):** UX research & information architecture, wireframes & prototypes, UI design & design systems, responsive/mobile design, developer handoff files.
- **Who this is for (80-100 words):** New product/MVP design, redesign of an existing product, design system creation for a growing team.
- **Process (180-220 words, 4 steps):** Discovery & research → Wireframes & flows → UI design & prototyping → Handoff & developer support.
- **Case studies (100-150 words + visuals):** UI/UX project thumbnails from /work.
- **FAQ (150-200 words, 4-5 Qs, FS target):** Tools used (e.g. Figma), whether development is included or design-only, typical timeline, how developer handoff works, revision process.
- **CTA (40-50 words):** Link to /contact.
