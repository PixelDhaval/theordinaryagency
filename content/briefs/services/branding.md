## Content Brief: branding design services

### Search Intent
Commercial. Searcher is evaluating agencies/studios to hire for a branding project - logo, identity, strategy. Knowledge level: ranges from founders who vaguely know they need "a brand" to marketing leads with a clear brief. Google rewards a service/landing-page format: value proposition, scope of work, process, proof, FAQ - not a blog-style guide.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** Competitors rarely spell out exactly what's *delivered* (file types, number of concepts, revision rounds) - most stay at the strategy/benefit level. Concrete deliverables build trust and reduce back-and-forth enquiries.
- **Depth gap:** Ramotion's 6-step process is thorough but generic/corporate. A tighter, plainer-language 4-step process (discovery → concept → design → handoff) is easier for a smaller client to follow and matches a minimal brand voice.
- **Quality gap:** Most competitor branding pages lean on scale-based proof (client logos, years in business, project counts). A studio without that history yet should lean on *specificity of craft* in case studies instead - real before/after, real reasoning - rather than vague trust badges.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Branding Design Services
**URL Slug:** /services/branding
**Target Word Count:** ~1,300-1,500 words (competitor avg ~3,500-4,200; shorter and more concrete rather than exhaustive)

- **Intro (60-80 words, primary keyword in first sentence):** What branding means at this studio and who it's for.
- **What's included (150-200 words, FS target: list format):** Concrete deliverables - brand strategy/positioning, logo and visual identity, color and typography system, brand guidelines document. Bullet list, not prose paragraphs.
- **Who this is for (80-100 words):** 2-3 short scenarios (new business launching, existing business rebranding, product line needing its own identity).
- **Process (200-250 words, H2 + H3 per step):** 4 steps - Discovery, Concept, Design & Refinement, Handoff & Guidelines. Each step: 1-2 sentences, no padding.
- **Case studies (100-150 words + visuals):** 2-4 relevant project thumbnails linking to /work/{slug}, filtered to branding work.
- **FAQ (150-200 words, 4-5 Qs, FS target):** Timeline, what's needed from the client to start, what happens after delivery (support/updates), difference between a logo refresh and full rebrand.
- **CTA (40-50 words):** Link to /contact.

Concrete deliverables and a plain 4-step process, backed by specific real case studies rather than scale-based claims (client counts, years in business) that a newer studio can't honestly make. ### E-E-A-T Requirements
- Case studies must be real projects with real outcomes described (what changed, why)
- Process description must match what actually happens in client work - no invented steps
- If timelines are stated (e.g. "4-6 weeks"), they must reflect real typical delivery times
- No fabricated client logos, review scores, or project counts
