## Content Brief: packaging design services

### Search Intent
Commercial, often with a transactional edge — searchers frequently already have a product and a looming production deadline. Audience: founders/brand managers for CPG, retail, or consumer products needing shelf-ready packaging. Google rewards a service/landing-page format with strong visual proof (packaging is highly visual) and clear process/pricing framing.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** Crème de Mint covers pricing transparency explicitly (starting price stated) — most competitors don't. Stating a realistic starting point or "request a quote" clearly (without a 9,000-word page around it) is a gap a minimal page can fill cleanly.
- **Depth gap:** Competitors rarely explain print-file/production handoff (dielines, bleed, color specs) in plain language — stating this builds confidence for first-time clients who don't know what to expect.
- **Quality gap:** The category's top result is bloated (9,000 words). A tightly-edited, visual-first page that still covers process and deliverables is a genuine differentiator for a "minimal and elegant" brand promise.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Packaging Design Services
**URL Slug:** /services/packaging-design
**Target Word Count:** ~1,300-1,500 words (competitor avg ~6,000+ skewed by one outlier; aim far shorter, visual-led)

- **Intro (60-80 words):** What packaging design means here — primary keyword in first sentence.
- **What's included (150-180 words, bullet list):** Structural/graphic design direction, concept exploration (e.g. 2-3 directions), print-ready files with correct bleed/color specs, dieline coordination.
- **Who this is for (80-100 words):** New product launch, rebrand/refresh of an existing line, multi-SKU product family.
- **Process (180-220 words, 4 steps):** Discovery & research → Concept directions → Design development → Production-ready handoff.
- **Case studies (100-150 words + visuals):** Packaging-specific project thumbnails from /work.
- **FAQ (150-200 words, 4-5 Qs):** Timeline, what files the client receives, whether printing/production is included or handoff-only, multi-SKU scaling, revision rounds.
- **CTA (40-50 words):** Link to /contact.

- Real packaging case studies with actual product photography, not mockup templates
- Accurate statement of what's included vs. not (e.g. clarify if structural/die-cut engineering or print production is out of scope)
- No fabricated retailer placements or sales-lift statistics unless verifiably true for this studio's own clients
