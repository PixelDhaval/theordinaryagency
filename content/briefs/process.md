## Content Brief: design process

### Search Intent
Informational/trust-building, mostly visited by prospects already considering the studio who want to know what working together looks like before booking a call. Low standalone SERP competitiveness for the generic term; this brief prioritizes clarity and conversion-support structure over ranking. Google format reward (where it does surface): step-by-step process page.

### Competitor Analysis
| # | URL | Key H2 Sections | Est. Words | Score | Main Gap |
|---|-----|-----------------|------------|-------|----------|
| 1 | sage.agency/process/ | Discovery, Design, Development, QA, Launch | ~600-900 | 24/40 | Written for web-design projects specifically; steps don't map cleanly to a multi-discipline design studio |
| 2 | artversion.com/about/process/ | Interdisciplinary approach across web/UI/branding | ~500-700 | 23/40 | Describes philosophy more than concrete steps/timeline a prospect can plan around |

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** Competitor process pages are usually written for one discipline (web design). A process page that explicitly shows how the same core process flexes across branding, packaging, pitch decks, social, brochures, and UI/UX is a gap specific to this multi-service studio.
- **Depth gap:** Most process pages skip timeline expectations per phase. Giving a rough duration per step (even as ranges) helps prospects plan and reduces early-stage back-and-forth.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** How We Work
**URL Slug:** /process
**Target Word Count:** ~700-900 words

- **Intro (60-80 words):** Why the process matters / what a client can expect working with the studio.
- **Step-by-step process (H2 per step, ~100-130 words each, 4 steps ≈ 450 words):**
  1. **Discovery** - goals, audience, scope defined
  2. **Concept** - initial directions/exploration
  3. **Design & Refinement** - iteration based on feedback
  4. **Delivery & Handoff** - final files, guidelines, support
  Note per step how it flexes slightly by service (e.g. packaging adds production file prep; UI/UX adds developer handoff).
- **What we need from you (80-100 words):** Brief/goals, brand assets if existing, timely feedback - sets expectations for a smooth project.
- **Timeline note (40-60 words):** Rough duration ranges per service type (link to relevant service pages for specifics).
- **CTA (40-50 words):** Link to /contact.

### Recommended Meta Tags

**Title**
Our Process | How We Work | The Ordinary Agency

**Meta Description**
From discovery to delivery - see exactly how a project with us works, step by step, before you book a call.

### Unique Angle and Information Gain
A single process framework shown flexing across six different services (rather than a generic one-discipline process page), plus honest timeline expectations - concrete planning information most competitor process pages omit.

### E-E-A-T Requirements
- Steps and timelines described must match how the studio actually works, not an idealized/invented version
- Any duration ranges given must be realistic, not marketing-inflated

### Internal Linking Opportunities
- Each service mention → its service page (anchor: service name)
- "See the process in action" → /work (anchor: "our work")
- CTA → /contact (anchor: "book a call")
