## Content Brief: contact design agency

### Search Intent
Transactional. Visitor has decided (or nearly decided) to reach out and wants the lowest-friction path to do so. Low SERP competitiveness for the generic term - this page is optimized for conversion rate, not organic ranking. Google format reward: short page, clear form/scheduler, minimal distraction.

### Competitor Analysis
| # | URL | Key Structure | Est. Words | Score | Main Gap |
|---|-----|---------------|------------|-------|----------|
| 1 | duck.design/book-a-call/ | Calendar booking widget, minimal copy | low | 28/40 | Clean booking-first approach but no fallback contact method visible for those not ready to book immediately |
| 2 | (category benchmark: contact-page best practice across agency sites) | Form + calendar hybrid, qualifying questions | varies | n/a | Many agency contact pages either over-qualify (long multi-step forms) or under-inform (no sense of what happens after submitting) |

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** Pages that only offer a scheduler (no fallback) lose visitors not ready to commit to a call slot immediately. Offering a scheduler as the primary path but a simple email/fallback contact as secondary covers both intent levels.
- **Depth gap:** Few contact pages set expectations for what happens after the visitor books or submits (e.g. "we'll confirm within 24 hours", "come with your project goals and timeline in mind"). This small addition reduces drop-off and anxiety.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Let's Talk
**URL Slug:** /contact
**Target Word Count:** ~250-400 words (minimal by design; the scheduler/form does the work)

- **Intro (40-60 words):** One or two lines inviting the visitor to book a call, primary keyword naturally worked in once ("get in touch with our design agency").
- **Booking scheduler (embedded, minimal surrounding copy):** Primary CTA.
- **What to expect (60-80 words):** Brief note on what happens after booking (e.g. a short intro call, no-pressure scope discussion).
- **Fallback contact (30-50 words):** Direct email and/or social links for those not ready to book a call.
- **Optional: quick service links (30-40 words):** Light links back to /services if the visitor arrived without a clear service in mind yet.

### Recommended Meta Tags

**Title**
Contact Us | Book a Call | The Ordinary Agency

**Meta Description**
Ready to start your branding, packaging, or design project? Book a quick call with our studio or reach out directly.

### Unique Angle and Information Gain
A dual-path contact flow (scheduler-first, with a clear low-friction fallback) plus a short "what happens next" line - closes the small but real anxiety/drop-off gap left by booking-only competitor pages.

### E-E-A-T Requirements
- Scheduler link and contact details must be real, working, and actively monitored
- "What to expect" claims (confirmation time, call format) must match actual studio practice

### Internal Linking Opportunities
- "Not sure what you need?" → /services (anchor: "see our services")
- "See our work first" → /work (anchor: "our work")
