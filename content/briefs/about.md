## Content Brief: about [agency name] design studio

### Search Intent
Mostly navigational/branded with a trust-building purpose rather than keyword-ranking purpose. Searchers (or clicking visitors) already have some awareness of the studio and want to confirm who's behind it before enquiring. Google format reward is minimal here - this page wins on conversion/trust, not organic ranking. Competitive landscape for a generic "about us" query is thin and not meaningful to chase; treat this as noted in Error Handling guidance (broaden focus to best-practice structure rather than SERP competition).

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Quality gap (common across the category):** Agency About pages frequently lean on vague mission language ("we believe in great design") instead of a specific, memorable origin story or point of view. A short, concrete story beats a generic mission statement.
- **Depth gap:** Many About pages separate "who we are" from "what we've done" - linking proof of work (case studies) directly from this page, rather than only on /work, keeps trust-building and evidence together.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** About [Agency Name]
**URL Slug:** /about
**Target Word Count:** ~700-900 words

- **Origin/story (150-200 words):** Why the studio exists, what founded it, what problem it was built to solve for clients. Keep specific and concrete, not generic mission-speak.
- **Philosophy/approach (120-150 words):** What "minimal and elegant" means in practice for how the studio works and what clients can expect.
- **Team (if applicable) (150-250 words):** Short bios/photos of founders or key team members - real names and roles, not stock "meet the team" filler.
- **Proof link (60-80 words):** Short line pointing to /work ("see what we've made") and /process ("how we work").
- **CTA (40-60 words):** Link to /contact.

### Recommended Meta Tags

**Title**
About Us | [Agency Name] Design Studio

**Meta Description**
Meet the studio behind the work - our story, our approach to minimal, elegant design, and the people doing it. See our process and get in touch.

### Unique Angle and Information Gain
Specificity is the information gain here: a real founding story, real names, and a real point of view on design beat the generic "passionate team of creatives" language used across most agency About pages.

### E-E-A-T Requirements
- Real names, roles, and (ideally) photos for anyone featured - no stock team photos
- Founding story should be accurate and verifiable, not embellished with invented years of experience or project counts
- Last-updated context isn't critical here, but team info should be kept current as the studio grows

### Internal Linking Opportunities
- "See our work" → /work (anchor: "our work")
- "How we work" → /process (anchor: "our process")
- "Get in touch" → /contact (anchor: "book a call")
